Tips for hard-to-read menus
  • Photograph the menu straight on, in good light, filling the frame. Take one photo per page or column.
  • Use Rotate if a page is sideways, and Invert colors for light text on a dark background.
  • Menu with lots of pictures? Use Select areas on the page and drag boxes around the text you want; only those parts are read. Strong image cleanup also helps.
  • Choose One column only if the items come out in the wrong order, or Paragraph layout if whole lines are missing.
  • Wrong letters or accents (French é, Spanish ñ)? Tick the menu's language under Languages and select Read again.
  • For a very decorative menu, a free AI chat app (such as Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini) can read it: attach the photo, paste the prompt below, then paste the reply into Or paste text or a table.

    Put your product photos online for free

    Meta and Google read photos from public links. One free way, with GitHub:

    1. Select Download photos (ZIP) and unzip it.
    2. On github.com, create a new public repository, for example shop-photos.
    3. Select Add file › Upload files and drop in all the photos. Commit.
    4. In the repository, open Settings › Pages, set Source to Deploy from a branch, choose main and / (root), and save.
    5. After a minute your photos are at https://<your-username>.github.io/shop-photos/. Put that address in Settings › Photo link address.

    Any public folder works the same way: your own website, or a hosting service. Products whose photos you added as links already have public photos.
    Free and open source. Text recognition by Tesseract.js, PDFs by PDF.js, spreadsheets by SheetJS.
