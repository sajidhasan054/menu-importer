The CSV uses UTF-8, so Bangla, Arabic, Chinese and other scripts open correctly in Excel and Google Sheets.

Tips for hard-to-read menus
  • Photograph the menu straight on, in good light, filling the frame. Take one photo per page or column.
  • Use Rotate if a page is sideways, and Invert colors for light text on a dark background.
  • Choose One column only if the items come out in the wrong order.
  • For a very decorative menu, a free AI chat app (such as Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini) can read it: attach the photo, paste the prompt below, then paste the reply into Or paste menu text or a table.
Text recognition by Tesseract.js, PDFs by PDF.js, spreadsheets by SheetJS.
